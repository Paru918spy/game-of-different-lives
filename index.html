A board game about walking in someone else’s shoes.

Why this game exists

Not everyone starts from the same place, and not every road is equally open. Many barriers are invisible to people who never meet them: a building without a ramp, a name that gets an application ignored, a rule that was never written with you in mind.

Facts and statistics rarely make us feel that. A game can. When you spin the wheel and are handed a life you did not choose, the unfairness stops being abstract.

Our mission

We want to build understanding, not pity. Every life in the game belongs to a real person who faced barriers, prejudice or doubt, or to a clearly marked fictional character, and every one of them also had skills, allies and goals of their own.

Our goal is that players leave with better questions: Who has to spin a three, and who simply walks on? What opens a bridge for someone? What could we change?

What you will find

  • 21 lives, 17 real people and 4 fictional characters, drawn by a wheel of fortune
  • Boards built from each person’s own story, with bridges, setbacks and barriers
  • Photos and sources from Wikimedia Commons, credited on every card
  • An ending with the real outcome, your own stats and questions to talk about

How we treat the stories

  • Respect first: no pity and no “inspiration” stories
  • Facts come from public biographies; illustrations are marked as such
  • Fictional characters are always labelled as fiction
  • The game takes about 15 minutes per life and works offline
