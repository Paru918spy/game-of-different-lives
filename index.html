    How does it work?
    1. On your turn roll the die and walk. The colour of the field you land on decides your card: a question about a person, or a real-life action.
    2. Answer right (or do the action) and you stay on the field. Wrong: your pawn goes back to where your turn started. On a ⚡ DOUBLE card a right answer moves you ahead again.
    3. Paths split. Choose your way: a longer trail with a bridge, or a short cut full of challenges.
    4. Reach the finish first to win.
