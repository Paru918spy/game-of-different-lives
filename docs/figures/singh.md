# Fauja Singh

*c. 1911 – 2025 · Age*

You are Fauja, a farmer's son in a Punjab village around 1911, with legs too weak to walk until you are five. Your running starts in your late 80s in East London, far from home, with a club and a coach who speaks your language.

## Documented events (what the game is based on)

Every effect on the board points to one of these events. If there is no documented event, there is no effect.

- **e1** (around 1911 to 1916): He could not walk until about five because his legs were weak. *Source: Khushwant Singh, The Turbaned Tornado*
- **e2** (childhood): He was a farmer's son in a Punjab village with almost no schooling. *Source: Khushwant Singh, The Turbaned Tornado*
- **e3** (2000s): He had no birth certificate, so Guinness World Records could not certify his age records. *Source: Guinness World Records statement on age records*
- **e4** (1992 onwards): After family losses he moved to Ilford in London, where a Sikh running coach and a club took him in. *Source: BBC and Guardian interviews*
- **e5** (2000): He ran the London Marathon for the first time at around 89, and finished marathons until he was about 100. *Source: BBC and Guardian interviews*

## Life stations (in the order of the road)

Every figure must stop at the 12 life stations on the way, one after the other. Effects: barrier = move back, boost = move forward; a question gives its bonus only for a right answer.

### 1. Punjab, around 1911  
**Story**

Fauja is born in a Punjab village. He has no birth certificate, so his exact age is never officially confirmed. His legs are so weak that he does not walk until about five.

### 2. Little schooling, no sport  
**Barrier (-1 fields)**

Other children tease him about his thin legs and he cannot join their games. He gets little schooling and no sport, and becomes a farmer like his family.

### 3. Family and farm  
**Boost (+1 fields)**

He marries Gyan Kaur and raises a family while working the land. In the village he is known as modest and kind, with neighbours he can rely on.

### 4. Losses and a new country  
**Barrier (-2 fields)**

His wife dies in 1992 and a son in 1994. After years of grief, Fauja moves to England to live with another son. In East London he is lonely, and the language is new.

### 5. A club in Ilford  
**Boost (+2 fields)**

In Ilford he starts running to cope with grief. At 89 he joins a running club and meets his coach Harmander Singh, a fellow Sikh, who also translates and becomes a friend.

### 6. "Too old"  
**Barrier (-1 fields)**

Doctors, strangers and even some friends are shocked and say a man his age should rest. People decide what age allows before they ask him. He trains daily, also in cold British weather.

### 7. The first marathon  
**Question (+1 fields if right)**

In April 2000 Fauja runs his first marathon in London. He finishes, and then goes on to run more marathons, including in New York and Toronto.

*Question:* About how old was Fauja Singh at his first marathon?

- ✔ 89
- 65
- 100

*Explanation:* He ran the London Marathon in 2000 at 89. He turned 100 (about) in 2011 and finished the Toronto Waterfront Marathon.

### 8. A record at 92  
**Boost (+2 fields)**

In 2003, aged 92, he sets a marathon record for his age group in Toronto. His coach, club and a community cheering at the side of the road are with him.

### 9. No paper, no record  
**Barrier (-1 fields)**

In 2011 he finishes the Toronto marathon at about 100, in just over 8 hours. Guinness World Records does not list it because there is no birth certificate. Many people still honour him, including with a letter from the Queen.

### 10. Torch bearer  
**Question (+1 fields if right)**

In 2012 Fauja carries the Olympic torch through London at about 100. He also takes part in advertising campaigns and supports charities.

*Question:* What did Fauja Singh carry through London in 2012?

- ✔ The Olympic torch
- The national flag
- A marathon trophy

*Explanation:* He carried the Olympic torch, and he used his fame to support charities.

### 11. A message for everyone  
**Story**

His motto: walk, eat simply, be positive. He says age is only a number but also stresses running safely. He ends marathons at 101 with a 10 km race in 2013.

### 12. Home in Punjab  
**Story**

Fauja Singh dies on 14 July 2025 after a road accident in his home village, at about 114 years old. He leaves a reminder: do not say "you are too old" before you have asked the person.
