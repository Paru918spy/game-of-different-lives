# Frida Kahlo

*1907 – 1954 · Chronic pain, disability, gender & Mexican identity*

You play a painter whose world is shaped by a body in pain and by hospital rooms. Long stretches of stillness cost you on the board, but a quiet corner and a mirror are where your best work happens. Every step you take is chosen, not rushed.

## Routes (zones)

The shared board has ten kinds of routes. For Frida, these differ from the normal rule (one field = one step):

| Route | Effect | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🪜 Stairway | slow (each field costs 2 steps) | Steep steps, no handrail for a weak leg and a damaged spine: the long way round takes time. |
| 🏁 Race track | slow (each field costs 2 steps) | A long run needs a body that is not in daily pain. Pacing costs steps. |
| 🌳 Quiet lane | fast (each field costs half a step) | Hours alone with an easel and a mirror: sustained focus is where Frida works at her best. |

All other routes work normally. The stone bridge, the long ramp, the quiet lane and the town hall can never be blocked, so there is always a way.

## Life stations (in the order of the road)

Every figure must stop at the 12 life stations on the way, one after the other. Effects: barrier = move back, boost = move forward; a question gives its bonus only for a right answer.

### 1. Coyoacán, 1907  
**Story**

Frida is born on 6 July 1907 in Coyoacán, near Mexico City, to a German-born photographer father and a Mexican mother. Later she says she was born in 1910, the year of the Mexican Revolution, to tie herself to the new Mexico.

### 2. Polio at six  
**Barrier (-1 fields)**

Frida gets polio at six. After months in bed her right leg stays thinner. Children call her "peg-leg Frida". Her father pushes her to swim and box, which builds the strength others said she would not have.

### 3. A girl among boys at the Prep School  
**Boost (+1 fields)**

Frida is one of very few girls at the National Preparatory School, where she studies hoping to become a doctor. She joins a clever, rebellious group of friends and learns to argue and laugh loudly.

### 4. 17 September 1925  
**Barrier (-3 fields)**

Aged 18, Frida's bus collides with a streetcar. A metal rail injures her badly: spine, pelvis, leg and more. Medicine in 1925 offers long bed rest and corsets. The plan to become a doctor ends. Over her life she has over thirty operations.

### 5. An easel above the bed  
**Boost (+2 fields)**

Her mother has an easel made that can be used lying down, and a mirror fixed above the bed. Frida paints her first self-portraits there. She says she paints herself because she is the subject she knows best.

### 6. What to paint in bed?  
**Question (+1 fields if right)**

In bed, with a mirror above her, Frida had to choose what to paint. Many of her best-known works come from this time.

*Question:* What became the main subject of Frida's paintings?

- ✔ Herself, in self-portraits
- Famous politicians
- Landscapes of Europe

*Explanation:* About a third of her paintings are self-portraits. She used them to show pain, identity and her Mexican heritage.

### 7. Diego, Mexico and Tehuana dress  
**Story**

Frida shows her paintings to the muralist Diego Rivera and marries him in 1929. She wears Tehuana dresses and fills her art with Mexican folk art and plants. Her style is her own, not a European copy or what a "woman painter" should do.

### 8. Henry Ford Hospital, 1932  
**Barrier (-2 fields)**

In Detroit Frida loses a pregnancy in a hospital that offers little comfort. She paints it, honestly and without decoration. Women's bodies and grief were rarely shown in art then, and critics found it shocking.

### 9. A first for Mexican art  
**Question (+1 fields if right)**

In 1938 Frida has her first solo show in New York. The following year, in Paris, a famous museum buys one of her paintings.

*Question:* Which museum bought a Frida Kahlo painting in 1939, the first work by a Mexican artist it acquired?

- ✔ The Louvre in Paris
- The Prado in Madrid
- The Tate in London

*Explanation:* The Louvre bought her self-portrait "The Frame" in 1939. It was a milestone for Mexican art.

### 10. Los Fridos  
**Boost (+2 fields)**

In the 1940s Frida teaches at an art school in Mexico City. When her health makes the trip hard, she teaches in her own garden. Her students, known as "Los Fridos", paint with her and share her view of art.

### 11. A leg is amputated, 1953  
**Barrier (-1 fields)**

Circulation problems lead to the amputation of her right leg below the knee. Medicine in 1953 has few options for her pain, and she is mostly confined to bed. She sinks into depression but keeps painting and keeps her friends close.

### 12. Casa Azul, 1954  
**Story**

Frida dies on 13 July 1954, aged 47, at the Blue House in Coyoacán. In April 1953 she had attended her first solo show in Mexico in her four-poster bed. Casa Azul opened as a museum in 1958 and her work is known around the world.
